## Individual editorial decisions

### Dorado

Explain the market, courthouse access and LumériCo separately. Give ground
access as an alternative to mandatory Winston/D.Va, without inventing exact
stairs coordinates. Replace fixed four-forward/one-pusher allocation with
remaining-enemy, coverage and checkpoint conditions. Distinguish displacing a
shooter from chasing them, and a last contest from waiting for another fight.
The two lineups describe follow-up and support vision, not mandatory picks.
The conclusion asks the reader to compare arrival timing on the second sector.

### Blizzard World

Separate the first capture, StarCraft escort and Diablo interior. A supported
touch can move the defense; clearing every height is not always required.
Remove forced composition swaps by phase and obligatory ultimate reservations.
Use the load's corner and Mei's wall to explain losing support vision. The
conclusion follows a Support through one turn instead of blaming all five picks.
Correct the original grammatical error `sobre el carga`.

### Nepal

Village explicitly puts the objective inside a building. Explain a wall that
cuts friendly healing, overlapping dive pressure at Shrine, and the direction
of displacement by Sanctum's pit. Percentage/last-touch conditions qualify
reset advice. Read player information before using the free replay camera.
Remove `minimapa mental`, `Haz una pregunta`, `mantén la forma del equipo` and
the artificial long-route exaggeration. The conclusion chooses one entrance
and compares help available before/after a wall or column.
