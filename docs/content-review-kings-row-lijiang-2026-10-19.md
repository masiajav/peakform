## Reasons for revising

King's Row prescribed spending exactly two compatible resources in the final
phase and treated saving for a later fight as an unconditional requirement.
Hotel, escort assignment and healing around bends needed different examples.
The revised article distinguishes the actual exit of hotel, the statue,
street turns, foundry holes and the last contest. Clock and available help
determine whether to regroup, touch or use an ultimate, not a fixed formula.

Lijiang prescribed a swap between rounds, an arbitrary 80 percent trigger,
waiting for a defensive ability before every crossing and a two-second
explanation for lost fights. Garden's environmental deaths were treated as
almost always the player's prior mistake. The revised Night Market, Garden
and Control Center sections distinguish each route and explain exceptions:
wall separation, available healing, bridge exit, contest deadline and what
the player's own perspective could actually show. Normal Control is clearly
distinguished from Stadium. These are conditional hypothetical ranked examples,
not personal playtest claims or Blizzard-endorsed compositions.

Both pages now have their own quick answer in the introduction, three useful

first-publication dates remain omitted. The earlier local sitemap-date fix
