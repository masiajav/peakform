# Midboss pit visual, Halloween 2026

The map article's Midboss chapter uses a new immutable detail image of the pit after the 30 October patch. It is an unmodified geometry render from Valve client build 6826, published by Frogtech, rather than an AI reconstruction or a screenshot from the old arena. Roofs are removed, materials simplified and entity markers visible.

- Original image: https://changelog.frogtech.dev/map-data/6826/shots/p1-after-hl.jpg
- Source and before/after: https://changelog.frogtech.dev/build-6826#map
- Extraction/rendering method: https://changelog.frogtech.dev/about
- Official patch: https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/1422450/view/680765564125186045

The map API still serves its previous minimap layers; their hashes were checked and unchanged. Those layers remain navigation overviews, not a current interior floor plan. The new detailed image and the article distinguish this explicitly. No crystal coordinates are inferred: Valve states their landing positions are random. Existing URLs, section indices, marker coordinates and the original publication date are preserved.
