# Next composition batch: preparation only

Source models for Ana, Genji, Kiriko and D.Va were read in full on 5 October
while the Tracer/Zarya regression suite was running. No public rewrite or
manual approval for those four is recorded by this document.

Their four rendered main areas and all five opened FAQ answers per page were
then read individually in the internal browser on the isolated 3021 preview.
This is baseline reading, not final editorial approval. The public paragraphs
match the model problems below; keep their original dates until rewriting.
Baseline main-area axe checks on the same preview cover four routes on two
viewports with five FAQ answers open: HTTP 200, no overflow, no WCAG violations
detected and noindex/follow on all eight checks. Evidence is in
reports/next-comps-baseline-2026-10-05.json. Passing those technical checks does
not resolve the editorial issues or authorise publication in the sitemap.

## Ana

Replace generic map categories with specific, explicitly hypothetical ranked
examples. Sleep requires landing the projectile, not simply identifying an
engage. Nano can help a necessary crossing or save an ally; contact-before-Nano
must not become an absolute rule. Healing with grenade can be the right choice.
Replace awkward headings such as "Brawl que cuida la mira" with a concrete
team plan. Add distinct VOD decisions rather than repeat the hero introduction.

## Genji

Remove the fixed half-second wait. Entry depends on actual help, resources and
reachable targets, not elapsed time. Low health does not guarantee a kill or
Dash reset. Tank initiation is useful but not an absolute prerequisite for
every Dash. Give each lineup actual map constraints and a fallback if a kill
does not happen. Review the Sombra substitution against the dated rework;
do not describe a future Support change as already live.

## Kiriko

"Dive con salida doble" is ambiguous and must not imply two free teleports.
The first ranked example includes two Tanks: label it 6v6 explicitly or make
it a consistent 5v5 example. Suzu can legitimately save an ally from damage;
holding it for another threat is conditional, not a rule against first damage.
Emergency healing is valid; avoid claiming every healing-only interval loses
the fight. Replace generic map categories and add specific VOD questions.

## D.Va

Remove fixed waiting intervals and vague "ambient" damage language. Explain
what Matrix can cover without implying it blocks every burst or beam. An
engage does not always require an enemy cooldown already spent; position,
health, simultaneous pressure and available help can justify it. Avoid blanket
rules against pursuing or initiating; distinguish the resources needed for a
return. Check dated substitutions and separate 5v5 from two-Tank examples.

concept article from 2022 is historical, not evidence for current balance:

records and official current pages before making new claims. No in-game test,
win-rate claim, invented cooldown number or blanket meta claim is authorised.
